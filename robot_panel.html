Poses

A pose stores every joint in degrees from home. To make one: move the joints (sliders, nudges), type a name, press Save current pose. To fine-tune: press Go, nudge the joints, then Update from robot, or type a number in the table and press Go again.

Build a position by typing degrees for each leg

Degrees from home: 0 = straight. Nothing moves when you save. Hips can go about ±40°, knees ±60°; bigger numbers are limited. Blank = leave that joint where it is.
